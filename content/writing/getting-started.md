# Setting Up the Workshop

Every hardware project starts somewhere. For me, it started with a folding table and a borrowed soldering iron.

## The Basics

A good workspace doesn't need to be expensive, but it does need to be *organized*. Here's what I consider essential:

- **Soldering station** — Temperature-controlled, with a good selection of tips
- **Multimeter** — Fluke 87V or equivalent
- **Oscilloscope** — Even a basic 4-channel DSO makes a world of difference
- **Power supply** — Bench PSU with current limiting (save your prototypes from themselves)

## Layout Matters

The physical arrangement of your bench directly impacts productivity. I follow a simple rule:

> Tools you use every session should be within arm's reach. Everything else goes in drawers.
